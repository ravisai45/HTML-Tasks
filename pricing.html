How Our Pricing Works

Cryptous uses a straightforward fee structure. We don’t hide extra charges or confuse you with complicated terms. You always see the fees before confirming any trade or transaction.

Fees can vary depending on your trading volume and whether you place maker or taker orders, but the core idea remains the same: fair pricing that is easy to understand.

💳 Deposit

Most crypto deposits are free. Network fees may apply depending on the blockchain.

🏦 Withdrawal

Withdrawals include a small fee that helps cover network costs and security checks.

🔁 Trading

Trades are charged based on your monthly volume and whether you are a maker or taker.
