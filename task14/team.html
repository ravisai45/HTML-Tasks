Our Team

Meet the people behind Cryptous — a mix of engineers, designers, traders and support specialists working together to build a better crypto experience.

People First, Always

At Cryptous, we believe that great products are created by teams that enjoy working together. Our culture is built on trust, ownership and continuous learning. We keep our structure lean so that good ideas can move quickly from discussion to reality.

🤝 Collaborative

Engineers, designers and product folks work closely with support and compliance teams.

🌱 Growing

We are small enough to move fast, but growing steadily with clear focus and direction.

🧠 Curious

We experiment, learn from data, and constantly improve both the product and ourselves.

Leadership

Our leadership team brings experience from traditional finance, technology startups and the crypto ecosystem. They are responsible for setting the vision and ensuring we move in the right direction.

Founder & CEO

Oversees company strategy, product direction and overall execution. Keeps the team aligned with our mission and values.

CTO

Leads the engineering organization, ensures platform performance, security and scalability.

Head of Operations

Manages support, compliance and day-to-day processes so users enjoy a smooth experience.

Teams at Cryptous

Every team plays a specific role in making sure Cryptous stays reliable, safe and easy to use.

Engineering

Builds and maintains the core platform — trading engine, APIs, security systems and user dashboards.

Product & Design

Defines how the platform feels, from new features to simple, clear interfaces for traders.

Support & Community

Helps users with onboarding, issues and feedback, and ensures we stay connected with our community.

Risk & Compliance

Works behind the scenes to keep the platform compliant and safe, following regulations and best practices.

Infrastructure

Ensures uptime, monitoring and reliability so markets remain available whenever users need them.

Learning & Content

Creates guides, updates and educational content to help users understand crypto better.
